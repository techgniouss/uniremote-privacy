# UniRemote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

UniRemote ("the app", package `dev.uniremote`) is an Android remote control and gamepad for TVs, set-top boxes and other devices, published by Mosaic Digital ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Contact: digivaultservice@gmail.com

## Summary

- We do not collect, receive or store any personal information.
- The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
- Your saved remotes and settings stay on your phone.

## What the app does

**Bluetooth.** The app connects to your TV or box as a Bluetooth keyboard/gamepad (HID) to send button presses. Bluetooth permissions are used only to find, pair with and connect to your own devices. The list of paired devices is read on your phone to show connection status and is not sent anywhere.

**Infrared.** If your phone has an infrared transmitter, the app sends IR codes from its built-in library when you tap a key. Nothing is recorded.

**Wi-Fi / local network.** The app may look for devices advertising services on your local network, to help you set up a remote. This happens only on your own network. The app does not send your data to us or to any outside server.

**Internet permission.** Android requires this permission for local network features. The app does not use it to contact our servers, and we operate none for this app.

**Saved remotes and settings.** Names, button mappings, layouts and preferences are stored only on your device. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes this information.

**Voice commands.** If you use the Voice feature, speech is handled by Android's built-in speech recognizer on your phone (provided by Google or your phone maker, under their privacy policy). The app receives only the recognised text and uses it to send a remote key. We do not receive or store your voice or the text.

**Home-screen widget.** The widget sends key presses to a remote you have saved, using the same local connections described above.

## Permissions

Bluetooth (find, pair and connect to your devices), infrared transmit, network and Wi-Fi state and multicast (local device discovery), and internet access (needed by Android for local network features). On older Android versions, Bluetooth scanning also requires the location permission; the app does not collect or use your location.

## Data sharing and retention

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties, because we do not receive any. Data on your phone is deleted when you remove a remote in the app, clear app data or uninstall.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
